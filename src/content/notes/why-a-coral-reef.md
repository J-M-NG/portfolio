---
title: "Why I framed my portfolio as a living coral reef"
date: "18 November 2026"
tag: "Product thinking"
projectSlug: "project-reef"
---

Inspired by the Forest app, I wanted a living visual metaphor where projects grow over time. Switching from a forest to a coral reef let the visual world harmonise naturally with J-M-NG/ui-kit blues, and unlocked bioluminescent dark mode.

Each coral documents the user story, problem, and learnings behind a vibecoded prototype rather than just listing frameworks.
